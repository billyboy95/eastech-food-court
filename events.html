What we plate

Kitchen with the rooms

Hire can be space-only or space with food from the campus kitchen. We plate from the same line that feeds students every weekday.

  • Buffet trays: stew, pap, rice, veg, chicken
  • Tea and coffee stations in the lounge
  • Braai packs on Fridays by arrangement
  • Graduation teas, Foundation days, workshop groups, SETA cohorts

Individual meal pre-order and checkout are not on this site. Send guests to thefood-court.co.za for that.
